Common questions

Which devices does LateFrame work on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

How does the delayed replay work?

Start Live replay and put the phone where it can see you. The camera keeps the last 2 minutes by default (30 seconds to 5 minutes in Settings). Swipe right to go back in time and left to go forward. Pause, slow motion and the frame wheel let you look at a skill frame by frame.

Where are my videos saved?

When you save, you choose Photos or LateFrame's library. The library is a folder on your iPhone that you can also open in the Files app, under On My iPhone › LateFrame. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

The camera or microphone isn't working

Check that LateFrame is allowed to use them in Settings › Apps › LateFrame. Without the microphone, clips are saved without sound.

Which video quality should I choose?

1080p at 60 fps is the default and a good balance. 120 fps gives smoother slow motion for fast skills, and uses more storage and battery.

My iPhone gets warm during long sessions

Filming for a long time warms any phone. If the phone gets hot, LateFrame lowers the frame rate to 30 fps until it has cooled down. Keeping the phone out of direct sunlight and off chargers helps.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
