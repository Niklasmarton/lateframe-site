Privacy policy

Your training stays on your phone.

Last updated 5 October 2026

LateFrame does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Video and sound are processed on your iPhone and never sent to us.

Who we are

LateFrame is made by Syverhuset Media (sole proprietorship, organisation number 933 617 947), Norway. Contact: support@lateframe.app.

Camera, microphone and photos

  • Camera: used to film the delayed replay. The replay is kept in a temporary buffer on your iPhone and discarded when you leave the live replay, unless you save a clip.
  • Microphone: used for sound in the clips you save and for your voice when you record an analysis. If you don't allow it, clips are saved without sound.
  • Photos: LateFrame only asks to add clips to your photo library when you choose to save there. When you import a video, you pick it in Apple's photo picker, and the app only receives the videos you choose.

Where your videos are stored

Saved videos are stored in LateFrame's own folder on your iPhone (visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › LateFrame) or in your photo library, as you choose. They may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups, depending on your own settings with Apple. We never receive them. Videos leave your phone only when you share them yourself, to the destination you pick.

Purchases and Apple

LateFrame is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and gives us sales reports that do not identify you. If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

When you email us

If you write to support@lateframe.app, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We keep the conversation as long as needed to help you and delete it when it's no longer needed. Our email is hosted by Hostinger.

This website

lateframe.app uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Its fonts are served from this site. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Filming others

If you film other people, for example teammates or gymnasts you coach, make sure they agree, and for anyone under 18, a parent or guardian. Recordings stay on your device and are your responsibility.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Since the app collects nothing, this will usually only concern emails you have sent us. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If LateFrame starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
